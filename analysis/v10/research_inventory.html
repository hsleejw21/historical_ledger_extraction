Research Inventory: Preserved Findings for Future Papers

Companion to analysis_v10_core.html (the leapfrogging core evidence package). Generated 2026-05-26 in response to advisor feedback.

Purpose: The Oxford ledger corpus is unusually rich and supports several distinct research questions beyond the leapfrogging core story. This inventory catalogues each preserved finding with its empirical support, methodological approach, and source artifact, so the archive can serve as a long-term research platform rather than a single-paper project.

How to fill in: The last three columns (Confidence, Theory angle, Future-paper direction) are deliberately left as TODO — they require theoretical judgment that belongs with the authors, not auto-extraction. Suggested ratings for Confidence: HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW.
